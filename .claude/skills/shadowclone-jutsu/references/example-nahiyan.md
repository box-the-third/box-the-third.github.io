## Clone 1: The Mentor

**Role:** IELTS Online Class Lead, YAS-beyond education.
**Origin: what I had to do:** Teach live classes for about 30 students worldwide, build the course (diagnostic test, weekly syllabus, timed mocks) and give Writing and Speaking feedback against the band descriptors.

**Superpowers**
- **Band-Whisperer:** turns a vague "your writing is weak" into the exact descriptor to fix. *Proof:* feedback built on official band descriptors; personal IELTS 7.5.
- **Syllabus Seal:** designs a course so students always know the next step. *Proof:* diagnostic, weekly plan and mock-test structure he built.
- **Exam-Day Calm:** coaches strategy and time management because he has sat the test. *Proof:* first-hand IELTS experience.

**Weakness:** Takes on too many students' emotions; needs boundaries around feedback hours.
**Summon when:** a learner is stuck on a plateau, a course needs structure, someone is nervous before exam day.
**Next mission:** collect two student results (with permission) to turn the class into proof.

## Clone 2: The Pathfinder

**Role:** Counselor Team Lead, Global Pathway Experts (Oct 2024 to Nov 2025).
**Origin:** Led study-abroad counselors, advised students on UK admissions and IELTS requirements, and wrote the SOPs the team ran on.

**Superpowers**
- **Map the Maze:** explains admissions steps so a nervous family can follow them. *Proof:* British Council UK Agent and Counselor certification; guided 1,000+ prospective students through the pipeline.
- **Team Seal:** turns one person's know-how into a process others repeat. *Proof:* SOPs and workflow automation cut asset deployment errors by 50%.

**Weakness:** Gets pulled into admin; the strategy gets squeezed.
**Summon when:** a process is messy, a team needs onboarding, a student needs a path.
**Next mission:** turn the SOPs into a one-page counselor playbook.
